About the model and people settings
MediaPipe (Lite, Full, Heavy)
Google's Pose Landmarker. Tracks 33 landmarks per person, including the face, hands and feet, plus an estimated depth for every joint. Lite is the fastest, Heavy the most accurate and slowest. All three can track several people, but MediaPipe has no memory of who is who: people are kept apart by position, so identities can swap when dancers cross or leave the frame.
MoveNet (Lightning, Thunder, MultiPose)
Google's TensorFlow.js model. Tracks only 17 joints (nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles) and no depth, so the overlay is simpler and the stick figure has no hands or feet. It is often faster than MediaPipe on phones and laptops without a usable GPU. Lightning and Thunder are single-person models; MultiPose tracks up to six people and has a built-in tracker that keeps identities stable.
People
How many dancers to track at the same time (up to six). Every extra person costs frame rate. The dashboard analyses each person separately, with one tab per person, and the exported file and CSV contain every person. Pose control starts or stops the recording when anyone raises both hands.
What does not change
All metrics use the same 13 major joints, which every model provides, so kinesphere, activity, speed and space are comparable between models. Each session records which model and how many people were used. Settings cannot be changed while recording; changing them re-initialises the detector (the first use of a model downloads it, from 5 to 30 MB).
